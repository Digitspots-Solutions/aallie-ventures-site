About Us

Is Blessed Foods Technology Limited (also known as Is Blessed AgriFoods Nigeria Limited) is a forward-thinking FoodTech company committed to revolutionizing Nigeria's agricultural landscape. Founded by visionary AgriTech leaders, we bridge the gap between traditional farming methods and modern technology to create sustainable food solutions. Our mission is to enhance food security, support local farmers, and deliver premium agri-food products that meet international standards while staying rooted in Nigerian excellence. We leverage innovative processing techniques, quality control systems, and efficient distribution networks to ensure that every product bearing our name represents the very best of Nigerian agriculture.

Our Services

Agricultural Technology Solutions

We provide farmers with access to modern farming tools, training, and tech-enabled farming practices that increase yield and reduce waste.

Food Processing & Packaging

Our state-of-the-art facilities process raw agricultural products into market-ready goods with extended shelf life and preserved nutritional value.

Quality Agri-Food Products

From grains and cereals to processed foods, we supply businesses and consumers with premium products sourced from trusted Nigerian farms.

Supply Chain Management

We connect farmers directly to markets, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair prices while maintaining product quality throughout the distribution chain.

Agricultural Training & Consulting

We empower farmers and agribusiness entrepreneurs with knowledge, best practices, and strategic guidance to build profitable, sustainable operations.
