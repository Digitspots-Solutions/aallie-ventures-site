Who We Are

Is Blessed Energy Resources Limited (also trading as Is Blessed Petroleum Services Co) is a Nigerian oil and gas service company founded and led by experienced petroleum engineers. We are headquartered in Jos, Plateau State, and serve clients across Nigeria's energy sector.

Our company combines international best practices with deep local knowledge to deliver reliable, cost-effective petroleum engineering solutions. We understand the unique challenges of operating in Nigeria's oil and gas industry and provide services that meet both regulatory requirements and operational demands.

With a commitment to safety, environmental stewardship, and technical excellence, Is Blessed Energy Resources Limited has built a reputation as a dependable partner for oil and gas operators seeking quality service delivery.

Comprehensive Petroleum Engineering Solutions

We offer a full spectrum of oil and gas services designed to optimize your operations:

Petroleum Engineering Consulting

Expert guidance on reservoir management, production optimization, and field development planning tailored to Nigerian geological conditions.

Drilling Services & Support

Technical supervision, drilling program design, and on-site engineering support for exploration and production drilling operations.

Production Operations

Well testing, production monitoring, artificial lift optimization, and facility maintenance to maximize asset performance.

Procurement & Supply

Reliable sourcing and delivery of quality oilfield equipment, consumables, and technical supplies with full compliance documentation.

HSE Management

Comprehensive health, safety, and environmental services ensuring compliance with DPR regulations and international standards.

Training & Capacity Building

Technical training programs for petroleum operations personnel, safety certifications, and skills development workshops.
